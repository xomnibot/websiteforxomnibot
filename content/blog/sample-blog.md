### Introduction

Breaking into vulnerability research requires consistency and hands-on practice. Here is the exact methodology I follow every week to keep expanding my skill set.

1. **Daily Hands-On Practice**: Solve 1 CTF machine or binary challenge per day.
2. **Read CVE Advisories**: Study patch diffs for published vulnerabilities.
3. **Build Open Source**: Convert manual exploitation scripts into reusable CLI tools.
